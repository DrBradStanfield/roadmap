## Decision for Brad (issue opened this run)

The fix is one constant in `app/lib/chat-router.server.ts:350`
(`callAnthropicWithUsage(body, 5_000)`). It is a latency trade-off, so it
is Brad's call:

- **A. 8 s a try, keep the retry.** Room for slow-but-alive tries. Worst
  case 17 s, only on a double stall. ±0 prod LOC.
- **B. Keep 5 s, drop the timeout retry.** Worst case 5 s. Loses the
  rescue seen 10-14 20:20Z. About +2 prod LOC (a `maxAttempts` argument).
- **C. Wait.** Keep counting. Output-token logging (branch
  `claude/router-output-tokens`) cannot explain a timeout: a timed-out call
  returns no usage. It still waits on the ALTER (column absent 10-16).

**Recommendation: A.** The rescued 8 s turn shows tries that stall past
5 s then succeed. 8 s covers that, and today's double stall already costs
11 s. I don't know how long the stalled tries would have run; A buys data:
if timeouts persist at 8 s, the stall is a hang, and B becomes right.
