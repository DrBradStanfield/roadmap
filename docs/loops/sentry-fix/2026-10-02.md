## A real defect, found in review, not yet tied to an event

pdfjs-dist ^4.9.155, with no polyfill (ES2024). `getDocument` throws on
Safari <17.4, Chrome <119 and Firefox <121. `extractWorker` swallows the
throw, so EVERY PDF ends in `no_files`. Our stated floor is ES2020 (we
fixed `Object.hasOwn` for iOS <15.4), and the ledger holds a Chrome 111
visitor (6J). This is 6W's leading candidate. I can't prove it: the event
has no browser.
