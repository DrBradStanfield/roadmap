## Proposals needing Brad (Tier 0 — propose only)

1. **Symptoms route to a treatment entry against rule 1.** Rule 1 allows a symptom to route only to
   sleep, or to a supplement the user blames. In 3-run arms the router still picks heartburn →
   alginates/DGL/manuka (3 cases), constipation → psyllium/magnesium (2), burning nerve pain →
   alpha-lipoic acid, chemo nausea → ginger, hospice constipation on fentanyl → psyllium (1/3),
   all-day pregnancy sickness → ginger (2/3), and post-coital bleeding → a cancer-tests post. Options:
   (a) a router-prompt trial that sharpens rule 1 ("a treatment entry is a diagnosis guess too"),
   measured on paired full arms in a build session; (b) accept it and re-point the fixtures;
   (c) accept it for adults, keep palliative and pregnancy ∅. **Recommend (a).** I may be wrong
   on the clinical side, but bulk laxatives are usually avoided in frail patients with opioid
   constipation, and an alginate answer can skip red-flag questions. The production rate is
   unknown: v4 has 17 router rows so far.
2. **YouTube vendor spam gets replies.** Add a classifier line: first-person vendor praise or a
   promotion is GREETING (no reply on YouTube). Measure it with the new fixture plus the 19 burst
   texts. The fixture is a KNOWN-FAIL until then. Recommend yes. A reply that names the vendor
   gives it free reach.
3. **CKD supplements** (backlog row): this is a clinical content decision. One user asked four
   times, and the lint pairs the algorithm's CKD protein rule (`round(IBW * 1.0)` at eGFR < 45) with
   protein-powders' "consult their doctor" line. That is W42's high pair; the entry's line has
   since changed, and the pair is now rated low and queued knowledge-side. The three-file sync applies if it is acted on.
4. **Variance 6.7% > 5% gate**: the harness exits 1 on a clean arm. Either re-baseline the gate
   for v4, or treat the gate as advisory until two v4 arms exist.
5. **Standing:** classifier harness needs prior turns (W42 #2); own-data asks ∅ (fifth week);
   router prose/parse shapes (W41 shape fixed; a prose-then-cap reply is new, one draw);
   W42 #3 classifier model per turn. **Closed:** W42 #1 (stale reflux fixtures: pathways gone), #7.
