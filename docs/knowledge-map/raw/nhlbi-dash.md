# DASH Eating Plan

Source: National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI). DASH Eating Plan.
URL: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/education/dash-eating-plan

## Composition (2,000-Calorie Diet)

### Daily Servings

| Food Group | Servings |
|---|---|
| Grains | 6–8 |
| Vegetables | 4–5 |
| Fruit | 4–5 |
| Meats, poultry, and fish | 6 or less |
| Low-fat or fat-free dairy products | 2–3 |
| Fats and oils | 2–3 |

### Weekly Servings

| Food Group | Servings |
|---|---|
| Nuts, seeds, dry beans, and peas | 4–5 |
| Sweets | 5 or less |

## Sodium Targets

- Standard target: 2,300 mg daily
- Enhanced target: 1,500 mg daily lowers blood pressure even further

## Key Principles

- Select foods low in saturated and trans fats
- Rich in potassium, calcium, magnesium, fiber, and protein
- Lower sodium levels
- Customisable worksheets available for 1,200; 1,400-1,600; 1,800-2,000; and 2,600 calorie levels

## DASH-Sodium Trial

Sacks FM et al. Effects on blood pressure of reduced dietary sodium and the DASH diet. N Engl J Med. 2001;344:3-10. DOI: 10.1056/NEJM200101043440101

The DASH-Sodium trial demonstrated that reducing sodium intake within the DASH diet further lowered blood pressure, with the greatest reductions in the group consuming the DASH diet with the lowest sodium level (1,500 mg/day).
