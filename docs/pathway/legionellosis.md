# Legionellosis

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways*

This pathway covers the assessment and management of Legionellosis, including Legionnaires' disease (severe) and Pontiac fever (mild). It is particularly relevant in New Zealand where soil and potting mix exposure is a common route of infection.

---

## What Is Legionellosis?

Legionellosis is an infection caused by *Legionella* bacteria. It presents in two forms:

- **Legionnaires' disease** — the severe form, causing pneumonia with high fever. Incubation period: 2–14 days.
- **Pontiac fever** — the mild form, causing flu-like symptoms without pneumonia. Resolves on its own. Incubation period: 5 hours to 3 days (usually 24–48 hours).

**There is no person-to-person transmission** — you cannot catch it from another person.

### How it Spreads

Two main routes:

1. **Inhaling aerosolised water** containing *Legionella pneumophila* — from cooling towers, hot tubs, spa pools, fountains, or water systems in hotels and hospitals.
2. **Soil and compost** containing *Legionella longbeachae* — **the most common route in New Zealand.** Disturbing potting mix, seed-raising mix, or compost can release dust that is inhaled.

---

## Who Is at Higher Risk

- Older age
- Smoking
- Immunosuppression (e.g., from medications or conditions like diabetes)
- Chronic lung disease

---

## Recognising Legionellosis

Think of Legionellosis in patients with an acute respiratory illness who have:
- Been exposed to potting mix or compost
- Prominent non-respiratory symptoms: headaches, abdominal pain, diarrhoea, nausea, vomiting
- Symptoms that are not responding to penicillin-based antibiotics

### Common Symptoms

- Dry cough
- High fever and chills
- Shortness of breath, chest pains
- Headache, excessive sweating, nausea, vomiting
- Abdominal pain
- Diarrhoea (more common than with other types of pneumonia)

---

## Severity Assessment — CRB-65 Score

Your doctor uses the CRB-65 score to assess severity (1 point for each):

- **C** — Confusion (new or worsening)
- **R** — Respiratory rate 30 or more breaths per minute
- **B** — Blood pressure: systolic < 90 mmHg or diastolic ≤ 60 mmHg
- **65** — Age 65 years or older

| Score | Severity | Approximate Mortality Risk | Typical Action |
|-------|----------|---------------------------|----------------|
| 0 | Low | < 1% | Community management likely appropriate |
| 1–2 | Moderate | 1–10% | Consider hospital referral |
| 3–4 | High | > 10% | Urgent hospital admission |

Social circumstances and available home support are also considered.

---

## Management

### When Hospital Admission Is Needed

Your doctor will consider urgent hospital admission if:
- CRB-65 score 2 or higher
- Oxygen saturation below 92% (or 3% or more below your personal baseline if you have existing lung disease)
- Signs of dehydration
- Worsening kidney function
- Significant other medical conditions
- Clinical concern

### Antibiotic Treatment

**Mild Legionellosis (CRB-65 score 0) — managed at home:**

For suspected Legionella (or if penicillin-allergic, or if atypical pathogen suspected):
- **Doxycycline** 200 mg immediately, then 100 mg twice daily for 7 days (10–14 days if atypical pathogen confirmed), or
- **Roxithromycin** 300 mg daily for 10 days

If Legionnaires' disease is specifically suspected, add roxithromycin and treat for a **total of 14 days**. Do not wait for test results before starting treatment.

Note: Avoid ciprofloxacin due to resistance concerns.

**Supportive measures:**
- Drink plenty of fluids
- Avoid smoking
- Simple pain relief for chest pain
- Monitor breathing and oxygen levels
- Control other conditions (e.g., blood glucose if diabetic)

### Follow-up

Your doctor should review you daily until you are stable or improving.

---

## Prevention — Potting Mix Safety

Because soil is the most common source of Legionella infection in New Zealand, your doctor may advise:

- Wear a **disposable face mask and gloves** when opening bags of potting mix, compost, or soil products
- Open bags carefully with scissors — do not rip
- Do potting **outside**, in a well-ventilated area
- **Dampen the mix with a sprinkle of water** before use to prevent dust
- **Wash hands thoroughly** after any contact with potting mix or soil

---

## Notification

Legionnaires' disease is a **notifiable disease** in New Zealand. Your doctor is required to report confirmed cases to Public Health authorities.

---

## Important Note

This pathway provides general information about how doctors assess and manage Legionellosis. Individual assessment, test ordering, and treatment decisions should always be made by your doctor based on your specific circumstances.
