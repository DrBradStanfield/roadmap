# Sexual Assault Medical and Forensic Assessment

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways*

This pathway outlines the specialist medical and forensic services available for patients who have experienced sexual assault or abuse — whether recently or in the past. Police involvement is **not required** to access these services.

---

## Services Available

### For Children and Young People (Under 18)

**Te Puaruruhau** is the regional health service for children and young people who have experienced abuse or neglect. It is located in a multi-agency centre together with Police and Oranga Tamariki, at 99 Grafton Road, Auckland (opposite the Starship building).

The service brings together the three statutory agencies involved in specialist child abuse investigation, with the aim of enhancing the recovery and care of those affected.

What Te Puaruruhau offers:
- 24-hour urgent medical service for acute abuse cases
- Nursing and social work assessments for physical abuse, sexual abuse, or neglect
- Referrals accepted for children and young people with disclosed or suspected physical or sexual abuse, or neglect

If there are concerns about a child or young person at risk, Oranga Tamariki (formerly Child, Youth, and Family) can be contacted 24 hours a day.

### For Adults (18 and Over)

**Pohutukawa Adult Sexual Assault Service** offers medical care and forensic medical examinations for adults (aged ≥ 18 years) who have been sexually assaulted, whether recently or in the past.

Who can access the service:
- Any adult who has been sexually assaulted — recent or historical
- Police involvement is **not required**

What the service offers:
- Privacy and confidentiality
- Discussion and advice for community providers regarding any case
- **Forensic medical examinations** — documenting injuries and collecting DNA or trace evidence for Police investigations
- **Non-forensic medical examinations** — specialist medical care without reporting to Police
- Emergency contraception (ECP) and antibiotic prophylaxis (to reduce STI risk)
- Referral and liaison with community services (counsellors, GPs, refuges, court support services)
- Family violence intervention and support

---

## What to Expect from a Referral

When a referral is made to the Pohutukawa Adult Sexual Assault Service, clinicians typically provide:

- Brief details of the alleged event, including date of occurrence
- Brief summary of any concerns about the patient
- Which other agencies are already involved
- The patient's current position regarding police involvement
