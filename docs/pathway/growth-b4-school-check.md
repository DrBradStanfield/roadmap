# Growth (B4 School Check)

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways*

This pathway covers growth measurement and management at the B4 School Check for children around age 4.

---

## Assessment

Your doctor or nurse will:

- Measure height and weight
- Calculate BMI for age and plot on a growth chart

---

## Management by Category

### Height and weight below the 0.4th percentile, or falling across 2 percentile lines

Your doctor may consider:

- Height of both parents (familial short stature)
- Other health factors such as chronic illness

Your doctor will arrange review every 3 to 6 months to monitor growth. If an underlying abnormality is suspected, a general practitioner assessment will be requested.

### Height and weight between the 0.4th and 91st percentile, no significant shift across centiles

This is a healthy weight range. No specific action is required at this stage.

### BMI at or above the 91st percentile

Your doctor may:

- Discuss concerns about unhealthy weight with the parent or caregiver, and explain the opportunity for the child to grow into a healthy weight
- Provide guidance on healthy eating and physical activity

### BMI at or above the 98th percentile

Your doctor will arrange a general practitioner assessment and follow-up.
