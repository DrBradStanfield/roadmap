# Finger Tip Units

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways*

This guide explains how to measure the right amount of topical cream or ointment using the finger tip unit (FTU) method.

---

## What is a Finger Tip Unit?

One finger tip unit (FTU) is the amount of cream squeezed from a standard tube with a 5 mm nozzle along an adult's index fingertip — from the tip of the finger to the first crease.

- **1 FTU = 0.5 grams** of topical cream
- 1 FTU covers approximately **2 hand prints** (1.6% of body surface area)

The amount required varies by body part. The tables below show how many FTUs are needed for adults and children.

---

## Adult Quantities

| Body Region | Surface Area (hand prints) | FTUs required | Quantity, twice daily for 2 weeks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Face and neck | 5 | 2.5 | 35 g |
| Trunk (one side) | 14 | 7 | 98 g |
| Entire trunk (front and back) | 28 | 14 | 196 g |
| One arm | 6 | 3 | 42 g |
| One hand and fingers (front and back) | 2 | 1 | 14 g |
| One leg | 12 | 6 | 84 g |
| One foot | 4 | 2 | 28 g |

---

## Paediatric Quantities (in FTUs)

| Age | Face and neck | 1 entire arm and hand | 1 entire leg and foot | Front trunk | Back trunk, including buttocks | Total body |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 to 6 months | 1 | 1 | 1.5 | 1 | 1.5 | 8.5 |
| 1 to 2 years | 1.5 | 1.5 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 13.5 |
| 3 to 5 years | 1.5 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3.5 | 18 |
| 6 to 10 years | 2 | 2.5 | 4.5 | 3.5 | 5 | 24.5 |
