# RSV (Respiratory Syncytial Virus) Immunisation

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways*

This pathway covers RSV immunisation options for adults and high-risk infants — who should be vaccinated, what vaccines are available, and what the evidence shows.

---

## Background

Respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) is a common cause of respiratory illness in infants, young children, and older adults. In older adults, RSV causes significant illness including lower respiratory tract disease, hospitalisation, and death.

Hospitalisation with RSV disproportionately affects Māori and Pacific peoples and those living in socio-economically deprived households.

---

## Vaccines Available

### Arexvy (Adults)

**Arexvy** is a recombinant protein vaccine for adults. It is **not funded** — patients pay for it themselves.

- Approximate cost: $340–$475 (including GST, courier, and practice fees)
- Available through pharmacy for adults aged **60 years or older**
- Available through general practice for patients aged **50 years or older**

### Palivizumab / Synagis (High-Risk Infants)

**Palivizumab (Synagis)** is a monoclonal antibody providing passive immunisation. It is **funded** on Special Authority for eligible high-risk infants during RSV season.

---

## Who Should Consider Arexvy (Adults)

Arexvy is approved for adults aged:

- **60 years or older**
- **50–59 years** who are at increased risk for RSV disease

Your doctor may consider Arexvy for people in high-risk groups, including those who:

- Have a chronic medical condition — respiratory, cardiovascular, neurological, neuromuscular (causing impaired airway clearance or respiratory muscle weakness), or haematological condition, diabetes, or a liver or kidney disorder
- Have obesity with a BMI of 40 or more
- Have moderate or severe immune compromise
- Are frail
- Are living in a nursing home or other long-term care facility
- Are of Māori or Pacific ethnicity, especially if living in an area of high deprivation

The risk of severe RSV increases with age — the highest hospitalisation rates are in adults aged 75 years or older.

---

## Palivizumab Eligibility (Infants)

Palivizumab is funded for infants who are:

- Younger than 12 months and born at less than 32 weeks' gestation

Or younger than 2 years with any of the following:

- Severe lung, airway, neurological, or neuromuscular disease
- Haemodynamically significant congenital heart disease
- Severe immunocompromise

**Dose:** 15 mg/kg by intramuscular injection every 4 weeks during RSV season (typically May to October).

Your doctor will arrange paediatric assessment if your child meets the criteria.

---

## Arexvy Efficacy

A single dose of Arexvy in adults aged 60 years or older provides:

**First season:**
- ~83% protection against RSV lower respiratory tract infection (RSV-LRTI)
- ~94% protection against severe RSV lower respiratory tract disease (RSV-LRTD)

**After two seasons (one dose):**
- ~67% (48–80%) against all RSV-LRTI
- ~80% (53–92%) against severe RSV-LRTD

**After three seasons (one dose):**
- ~63% against RSV-LRTI

---

## Dosing and Timing

- A single-dose primary course of Arexvy is given at the beginning of RSV season (usually winter)
- Further data is still needed to determine the optimal timing of revaccination

**If you have already had RSV this season:** People can get RSV more than once in a season, but a second infection is usually less severe. Vaccination late in the season may not be necessary if you have already had RSV that season — though vaccination can still be considered if RSV occurred early in the season. Discuss with your doctor.

---

## Co-administration with Other Vaccines

Arexvy contains a novel adjuvant to enhance the immune response. Your doctor may advise you about stronger post-vaccination responses if Arexvy is given alongside other adjuvanted vaccines.

- **Shingrix (shingles vaccine)** contains the same adjuvant as Arexvy. Studies show no differences in immune response with co-administration, but mild to moderate reactions (e.g. fatigue, injection site pain) may occur more frequently.
- **Fluad Quad (influenza vaccine for 65+)** contains a different adjuvant. Your doctor will inform you of the possibility of a stronger post-vaccination response if these are co-administered.
