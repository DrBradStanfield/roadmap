# Hair Loss Subsidy

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways*

A government subsidy is available to help cover the cost of a wig, hairpiece, or headwear if you have a medical condition that has caused hair loss — for example, cancer (including chemotherapy) or alopecia.

---

## Who Is Eligible

Anyone with a medical condition that has caused hair loss may be eligible. This includes conditions such as:

- Cancer (e.g., hair loss from chemotherapy)
- Alopecia

---

## How Much Is Available

Payment amounts depend on whether the hair loss is permanent or temporary:

- **Temporary hair loss:** lower payment tier
- **Permanent hair loss:** higher payment tier
- Payment ranges from **$400 to $2,330**
- For patients **under 18 years:** the payment is **$1,226**

---

## How to Claim

You have two options:

1. The **supplier** can make a claim on your behalf directly to the Ministry of Health.
2. You **pay first**, then claim reimbursement yourself.

---

## What Your Doctor Needs to Provide

To apply, you need a **medical certificate** from your GP or specialist. This must include:

- Your NHI number
- Details of the hair loss condition
- Whether the hair loss is **permanent or temporary**

Ask your doctor to complete this at your appointment. The certificate is then used to support your subsidy application.

---

## Applying for the Subsidy

Applications go to:

**Disability Services, Ministry of Health**
Phone: **0800 458-448**

Your doctor or supplier can advise on the process. Always discuss your eligibility and next steps with your doctor or specialist.
