# Mothers, Babies, and Parenting Community Support

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways*

This guide covers community support services available for mothers, parents, and whānau — including helplines, parenting education, mental health support, food assistance, and pregnancy loss support.

If you are unsure which service is right for you, discuss your needs with your doctor or midwife.

---

## Helplines — Available 24/7

These free national helplines are available any time:

- **1737 Need to Talk** — Free call or text **1737** for trained counsellors. [1737.org.nz](https://www.1737.org.nz/)
- **PlunketLine** — Free parenting helpline for families and caregivers. Phone **0800 933 922**. [plunket.org.nz](https://www.plunket.org.nz/plunket/what-we-offer/plunketline/)
- **Healthline NZ** — Free healthcare advice. Phone **0800 611 116**. [healthy.org.nz](https://www.healthy.org.nz/)
- **Mothers Helpers NZ** — Support and advice for mothers under stress. Phone **0800 002 717**. [mothershelpers.co.nz](http://mothershelpers.co.nz/)

---

## Finding a Midwife

If you are pregnant and need a lead maternity carer (LMC/midwife):

- [Find Your Midwife](https://www.findyourmidwife.co.nz) — New Zealand College of Midwives directory
- [Healthpoint Midwifery Services](https://www.healthpoint.co.nz/maternity/midwife-practice/)

If you are having difficulty finding a midwife, let your doctor know — they can help with a referral.

## Antenatal Classes

Antenatal education can help you prepare for birth and early parenting:

- **MAMA Maternity** — Information and services for parents-to-be. [mamamaternity.co.nz](http://www.mamamaternity.co.nz/)
- **Multiples NZ** — Parent-led support for those expecting more than one child. [multiples.org.nz](https://multiples.org.nz/)
- **Parents Centre Aotearoa** — Antenatal and parenting courses. [parentscentre.org.nz](https://parentscentre.org.nz/)
- **Plunket** — Online antenatal education. [Plunket Antenatal](https://www.plunket.org.nz/plunket/what-we-offer/antenatal-education/)

## Parenting Education and Support

- **Parenting Place** — Online resources, family coaching, and events. [parentingplace.nz](https://parentingplace.nz/)
- **Playcentre** — Parenting and early childhood education. [playcentre.org.nz](http://www.playcentre.org.nz/)
- **Plunket** — Parenting education, support, and connection. [Parenting Education](https://www.plunket.org.nz/plunket/what-we-offer/parenting-education-and-support/)
- **Triple P (Positive Parenting Programme)** — Evidence-based programme for managing difficult child behaviour, from preschool to teenage years. Available online and in-person. [triplep-parenting.net.nz](https://www.triplep-parenting.net.nz/nz/triple-p/)
- **Barnados** — Home visits, advocacy, parenting courses, and family support programmes. [barnardos.org.nz](http://www.barnardos.org.nz/)
- **Family Start (Oranga Tamariki)** — Home visiting programme for families to improve children's health and relationships. [Oranga Tamariki: Family Start](https://www.orangatamariki.govt.nz/support-for-families/support-programmes/family-start/)
- **Grandparents Raising Grandchildren** — Support for grandparents raising grandchildren full-time. [grg.org.nz](https://www.grg.org.nz/)

## Mental Health Support During Pregnancy and Postpartum

If you are struggling with your mental health during pregnancy or after birth, please talk to your doctor or midwife. They can help connect you with the right service.

- **Yellow Brick Road** — Free mental health and addiction support for family/whānau. Phone **0800 732 825**. [yellowbrickroad.org.nz](https://yellowbrickroad.org.nz/)
- **Dayspring Trust** — Psychosocial support for women with young children. Phone **(09) 827 6321** or self-refer. [dayspring.co.nz](http://www.dayspring.co.nz/)
- **Community Alcohol and Drug Service (CADS)** — Assessment and support for pregnant women and parents of children under 3. Phone **(09) 845 1818**. [cads.org.nz](https://www.cads.org.nz/services/pregnancy-and-parental/)

Your doctor may also refer you to specialist perinatal mental health services if needed.

## Sleep Advice for Parents

Online resources for parents needing help with their baby or child's sleep:

- **Healthify** — [Sleep for Babies and New Parents](https://healthify.nz/hauora-wellbeing/s/sleep-for-babies-and-new-parents), [Sleep Deprivation and Parenting](https://healthify.nz/hauora-wellbeing/s/sleep-deprivation-and-parenting)
- **KidsHealth** — [Sleeping Sound](https://www.kidshealth.org.nz/topics/managing-sleep-problems) — tools for managing sleep problems in children
- **Plunket Sleep Resources** — Guides from newborn through to 5 years: [plunket.org.nz/child-development/sleep](https://www.plunket.org.nz/child-development/sleep/)
- **Raising Children** — [Sleep resources](https://raisingchildren.net.au/newborns/sleep/understanding-sleep/about-sleep) for all ages
- **Parenting Place** — [Sleep resources](https://parentingplace.nz/?s=sleep) for all ages

## Food and Budgeting Support

If your family needs help with food or finances:

- **Bellyful** — Free meal delivery for families with new babies (usually under 3 months) or young children with serious illness. Phone **0508 235 5938** or email [contact@bellyful.org.nz](mailto:contact@bellyful.org.nz). [bellyful.org.nz](https://bellyful.org.nz/)
- **Kore Hiakai Zero Hunger Collective** — Directory of food banks, community meals, fruit and vege co-ops, and food rescue services. [zerohunger.org.nz](https://www.zerohunger.org.nz/find-a-community-food-organisation)
- **Foodbank New Zealand** — Searchable directory of food banks across NZ. [foodbank.co.nz](https://www.foodbank.co.nz/)
- **Salvation Army Community Services** — Food assistance, budgeting, and family support. [salvationarmy.org.nz](https://www.salvationarmy.org.nz/)

---

## Pregnancy Loss Support

If you have experienced miscarriage, stillbirth, or infant loss:

- **Miscarriage Support** — Support and information for women who have lost a child. Phone **0508 726 372**. [miscarriagesupport.org.nz](https://www.miscarriagesupport.org.nz/)
- **SANDS** — Support for parents and families who have lost a baby in pregnancy, around birth, or as an infant. Phone **0508 726 372** or email [info@sandsauckland.org.nz](mailto:info@sandsauckland.org.nz). [sandsauckland.org.nz](http://www.sandsauckland.org.nz/)

If you are in crisis outside of hours, contact **1737** (free call or text) for immediate support.

---

## Well Child / Tamariki Ora

The **Well Child Tamariki Ora** programme provides free health checks for all children under 5. This includes 12 core contacts and a GP check at 6 weeks. Your Plunket nurse or doctor can guide you through this.

---

## Baby Equipment

- **Pregnancy Help** — Walk-in, free confidential services including free pregnancy tests, clothing, and baby equipment. [pregnancyhelp.org.nz](http://www.pregnancyhelp.org.nz/auckland/12/)
- **St Vincent de Paul** — Baby clothes, bedding, and bassinets. [svdp.org.nz](https://www.svdp.org.nz/need-help/)

For safe sleeping equipment (pepi-pod or wahakura), ask your doctor or midwife.
