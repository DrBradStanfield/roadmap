# Exudate and Moisture Balance in Wounds

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways*

This pathway covers the assessment and management of wound exudate and moisture balance to support optimal wound healing.

---

## Background

- Moisture balance is a delicate process in wound healing.
- Excessive moisture causes moderate to high levels of exudate which can damage the skin surrounding a wound, leading to maceration and delayed healing.
- Inadequate moisture in the wound environment can delay healing and cause eschar formation.

---

## Assessment

Your doctor or nurse will examine:

- **Level of exudate** — how much fluid is produced by the wound
- **Colour and viscosity** of the exudate
- **Condition of surrounding skin**, including:
  - **Maceration** — caused by prolonged exposure to moisture and wound exudate on the surrounding skin, resulting in the area becoming water-logged. Ensure dressing absorbency and frequency of changes are adequate.
  - **Excoriation** — injury to the skin (e.g., scratch or abrasion), which may be caused by repeated use of adhesive tapes or dressings.

---

## Management

Your doctor or nurse may consider the following steps:

1. **Treat the underlying cause** — for example, lower limb oedema.
2. **Remove exudate** by wicking away from the wound bed.
3. **Choose a dressing** based on the level of exudate and the moisture balance of the wound:

### Low Exudate

Wounds can become dry. Options include:

- **Simple dressings** such as fine tulle (e.g., Cuticerin, Adaptic)
- **Hydrocolloid** (e.g., Comfeel, Hydrocol) — helps maintain moisture balance and protect the wound for longer
- **Gels** (e.g., Intrasite, Solosite) — used to hydrate dry wounds, help lift eschar off the wound bed, and for autolytic debridement of slough. Note: do not use on ischaemic limbs (must be kept dry); review regularly as wounds can become too moist with gels
- **Film** (e.g., Tegaderm, Opsite) — protects from water exposure

### Moderate or High Exudate

- **Gel-forming dressings** such as alginates (e.g., Algisite M) for moderate exudate
- **Foams** (e.g., Mepilex, Allevyn, Aquacel foam, Tegaderm foam) — monitor to ensure they do not become saturated, which can lead to skin damage
- **Super absorbent dressings** (e.g., DryMax, Vliwasorb, Relevo, Mextra) — expand to absorb heavily and can be used as a primary or secondary dressing depending on the wound
- For very heavy exudate, change dressings more frequently where possible

4. **Specialist wound products** — for heavily exudating wounds, negative pressure wound therapy (NPWT) using products such as VAC or Renasys may be necessary. Your doctor will arrange referral for specialised wound care if needed.

## Dressing Reference Table

| Product | Indications | Precautions |
|---|---|---|
| Absorbent pad (non-wicking) e.g., combine | Soft and absorbent | Avoid on very heavily exudating wounds — once saturated, exudate will sit on the wound and macerate surrounding tissue |
| Alginate rope e.g., Algisite rope, Kaltostat rope, Sorbalgon rope, Melgisorb rope | Calcium alginate (seaweed derivative). Transforms absorbed exudate into a gel. Haemostatic. Gently fills cavities. | Do not use on dry wounds. Ensure packed material can be retrieved. |
| Alginate sheet e.g., Algisite M, Kaltostat, Sorbalgon, Melgisorb, Tegaderm alginate | Calcium alginate. Absorbs exudate transforming into a gel. Cut to size of wound. Haemostatic. | Do not use on dry wounds. Ensure packed material can be retrieved. |
| Foams e.g., Mepilex, Allevyn, Aquacel foam, Tegaderm foam, Advasorb, PolyMem, PermaFoam | Low adherent polyurethane absorbent dressing. Absorbs low to moderate exudate. Overlap wound by 2 cm. Use adhesive border unless under bandaging. | Avoid on very heavily exudating wounds — once saturated, exudate will macerate surrounding tissue. |
| Hydrofibre rope e.g., Aquacel rope, Durafiber rope, Exufiber rope | Absorbs exudate transforming into a gel | Do not use on dry wounds. Ensure packed material can be retrieved. |
| Hydrofibre sheet e.g., Aquacel, Durafiber, Exufiber | Absorbs exudate transforming into a gel. Provides vertical wicking to protect immediate wound surroundings — overlap wound margins. | Do not use on dry wounds. Ensure packed material can be retrieved. |
| Super absorbent dressings e.g., Relevo, Mextra super absorbent | Highly absorbent pads used mainly as a secondary dressing to wick exudate away from the wound and surrounding tissue. | Do not cut these products — this interferes with absorbency and can result in particles leaking into the wound. |

---

Always discuss wound care management with your doctor or nurse, who can assess your specific wound and recommend the most appropriate treatment.
