# Non-acute Neurosurgery Assessment

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways*

This pathway covers which conditions are appropriate for non-acute neurosurgery referral and what alternative pathways apply when neurosurgery is not the right service.

---

## Conditions Seen by Neurosurgery

Non-acute neurosurgery assessment is appropriate for:

- **Hydrocephalus** — including normal pressure hydrocephalus and idiopathic intracranial hypertension
- **Severe pain** — e.g., trigeminal neuralgia
- **Intracranial and spinal tumours**
- **Aneurysms, AV malformations, and other cerebrovascular lesions**
- **Chiari malformations**
- **Syringomyelia**
- **Congenital spinal anomalies**
- **Peripheral nerve decompression** — for acute entrapment where there is a significant neurological deficit or pain
- **Surgery for epilepsy**
- **Surgery for movement disorders**
- **Spinal conditions with neurological deficit** (in Auckland only)

---

## Conditions Not Seen by Neurosurgery — Alternative Pathways

Some conditions are directed to other services:

- **Craniosynostosis** — refer to non-acute plastic surgery assessment instead
- **Spinal conditions with neurological deficit** (in Counties Manukau and Waitemata regions) — refer to non-acute orthopaedic assessment instead

---

## Key Points

- This pathway covers **non-acute** referrals — for acute neurosurgical emergencies, a separate acute pathway applies
- Whether spinal conditions with neurological deficit go to neurosurgery or orthopaedics depends on the DHB region (Auckland → neurosurgery; Counties Manukau and Waitemata → orthopaedics)
- Private neurosurgery is also available — contact a private provider directly if the patient prefers or is not eligible for public funding
