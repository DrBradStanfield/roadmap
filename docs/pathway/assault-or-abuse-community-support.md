# Assault or Abuse — Community Support

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways (adapted for national use)*

This page covers community support services for family or interpersonal violence, elder abuse, refuge services, and sexual assault in New Zealand. Services marked as Auckland-specific or with local (09) numbers only have been omitted — nationally accessible services are listed below.

---

## National Helplines

- **[1737 Need to Talk](https://www.1737.org.nz/)** — Free call or text **1737** (24 hours, 7 days). 24/7 trained counsellors.

- **[Lifeline](https://www.lifeline.org.nz/services/)** — Phone **0800 543 354** (24 hours, 7 days). Confidential support from qualified counsellors and trained volunteers.

- **[Women's Refuge](https://womensrefuge.org.nz/)** — Phone **0800 733 843** (24 hours, 7 days). Supports women and children experiencing family violence, including home visits and refuge housing.

- **[Shine Helpline](https://2shine.org.nz/what-we-do/shine-helpline)** — Phone **0508 744 633**. Phone-based self-help information, support workers for home visits, refuge and transitional housing. Resources for children, adults, and medical practices.

- **[Shakti](https://www.shakti.org.nz/)** — Phone **0800 742 584** (24 hours, 7 days). Women's centre and refuge for Middle Eastern, Latin American and Asian (MELAA) women.

- **[Rape Prevention Education (RPE)](https://rpe.co.nz/find-your-local-support-service/)** — Phone **0800 044 334** (24 hours, 7 days). National collective of rape crisis and related support groups.

- **[Victim Support NZ](https://www.victimsupport.org.nz/)** — Phone **0800 842 846**. Free support to victims of serious crime and trauma.

- **[Family Violence It's Not OK](https://www.areyouok.org.nz/)** — Phone **0800 456 450**. Resources and referrals for people experiencing or concerned about family violence.

- **[Youthline](https://youthline.co.nz/)** — Phone **0800 376 633** or free text **234**. Counselling, mentoring, youth health services and employment support.

- **[What's Up](https://whatsup.co.nz/)** — Phone **0800 942 8787**. Free counselling for children and youth, with online chat.

- **[Asian Family Services](https://www.asianfamilyservices.nz/)** — Phone **0800 862 342**. Counselling in English, Mandarin, Cantonese, Korean, Vietnamese, Japanese, Thai, and Hindi. Interpreter available for other languages.

- **[Barnardos](https://barnardos.org.nz/)** — Phone **0800 227 627**. Family support including housing, budgeting, parenting skills and counselling.

- **[Oranga Tamariki](https://www.orangatamariki.govt.nz/)** — Phone **0508 326 459**. Ministry for Children — support services for families and children.

---

## Support for Men and Boys

- **[Better Blokes](https://betterblokes.org.nz/)** — Phone **0800 448 484**. Peer support and workshops for male survivors.

- **[Man Alive Tāne Ora](https://www.manalive.org.nz/)** — Phone **0800 826 367**. Individual and couples counselling, youth programmes, Men in Relationships programme, and Men's Change and Support groups.

---

## Elder Abuse

- **[Age Concern New Zealand](https://www.ageconcern.org.nz/)** — Freephone **0800 652 105** (all areas). Services for older people including elder abuse and neglect support, health promotion, falls prevention, and driver refresher courses.

---

## ACC Sensitive Claims

People who have experienced sexual assault or abuse can lodge an ACC sensitive claim. ACC covers treatment and support costs.

- **ACC Sensitive Claims** — Phone **0800 735 566** or email [sensitiveclaims@acc.co.nz](mailto:sensitiveclaims@acc.co.nz)
- [FindSupport](https://www.findsupport.co.nz/) — ACC-funded directory to find a local support service

---

## Legal Support

- **[Legal Aid](https://www.justice.govt.nz/courts/going-to-court/legal-aid/)** — Phone **0800 253 425**. Government funding to help people who cannot afford a lawyer, including for protection orders and family court matters.

---

## Stopping Violence — Programmes for Perpetrators

For people who want to stop using violent or controlling behaviour:

- **[SafeMan SafeFamily](https://safemansafefamily.org.nz/)** — Phone **0800 SAFEHELP (0800 723 4357)**. Peer-led, professionally supported programme to help men end the cycle of family violence.

- **[Shine — No Excuses Programme](https://2shine.org.nz/what-we-do/non-violence-programmes/)** — Phone **0508 744 633**. Non-violence programme for male perpetrators.

---

## Finding a Counsellor or Psychologist

National registers to find a qualified mental health professional:

- **[New Zealand Association of Counsellors (NZAC)](https://nzac.org.nz/client/roster/clientRosterView.html?clientRosterId=677)** — Largest professional body representing counsellors in New Zealand.

- **[New Zealand Association of Psychotherapists (NZAP)](https://nzap.org.nz/member-directory#!directory/map)** — Register of psychotherapists.

- **[New Zealand College of Clinical Psychologists (NZCCP)](https://www.nzccp.co.nz/for-the-public#find-a-psychologist)** — Find a clinical psychologist.

- Migrant and refugee support
- Māori health services
- Pacific health services
- Child, parenting, and youth community support
- Youth mental health counselling
- Adult mental health counselling

*Always discuss your situation with your doctor or a health professional. If you or someone is in immediate danger, call 111.*
