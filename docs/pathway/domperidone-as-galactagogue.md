# Domperidone as Galactagogue

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways*

This pathway covers the use of domperidone as a medication to help increase breast milk supply (a "galactagogue").

---

## What is Domperidone?

Domperidone is a medication primarily used to treat gastro-oesophageal reflux and upper gut motility disorders. A side effect of domperidone is that it raises prolactin levels in the blood — prolactin is the hormone that stimulates breast milk production. This is why it is sometimes considered as a galactagogue (a medication to boost milk supply).

**Domperidone is not registered for use as a galactagogue in New Zealand.** Its use for this purpose is off-label, meaning your doctor is prescribing it outside of its officially approved indications.

---

## Evidence: Benefits

- Some studies, including small randomised controlled trials, suggest domperidone can increase breast milk supply compared to placebo.
- However, the evidence is not high quality. There is no strong evidence that domperidone is more effective than non-medication approaches — such as increasing the frequency of breastfeeding and improving breastfeeding technique.
- Your doctor will likely recommend optimising non-pharmacological support first before considering domperidone.

---

## Evidence: Risks

- Domperidone has been associated with a small theoretical risk of serious heart rhythm problems (ventricular arrhythmia) and sudden cardiac death — however, this risk applies mainly to older patients on higher doses for gut conditions, not lactating women.
- Risk factors for cardiac adverse events in breastfeeding women are largely theoretical and not well supported by scientific evidence.
- No cases of sudden death or serious cardiac adverse effects have been reported in studies specifically assessing domperidone's use as a galactagogue.

---

## Your Doctor May Consider

If you and your doctor decide to try domperidone as a galactagogue:

- A full discussion of the risks and benefits, so you can make an informed decision
- Checking for any personal cardiac risk factors before prescribing
- Prescribing at the lowest effective dose for the shortest time necessary
- Reviewing the NZ Formulary entry for [domperidone](https://nzf.org.nz/nzf_2382) for full prescribing information
