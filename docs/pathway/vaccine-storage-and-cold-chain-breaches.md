# Vaccine Storage and Cold Chain Breaches

*Source: Auckland Region HealthPathways*

This pathway covers the requirements for vaccine cold chain maintenance and the steps to follow when a cold chain breach occurs.

---

## Background

The cold chain is the system of ensuring vaccines are transported and stored at **2 to 8°C at all times**, from the place of manufacture until administration.

Maintaining the cold chain ensures vaccine potency. The success of an immunisation programme depends on vaccine potency and is the responsibility of all health professionals involved in vaccine transport, storage, or administration.

---

## Management

### Routine Procedures

Your healthcare provider should ensure good procedures are in place for monitoring and documentation of fridge temperatures on an ongoing basis.

### If a Cold Chain Breach Occurs

A cold chain breach is defined as the fridge temperature going outside the **2 to 8°C range**. If this occurs, your healthcare provider will typically:

1. **Label and quarantine** the affected vaccines immediately — do not discard or use them until advised.
2. **Document the incident carefully**, including the duration and extent of the temperature excursion.
3. **Review the refrigerator temperature records** and download data logger information.
4. **Contact a local immunisation coordinator** for advice on whether affected vaccines can still be used.

Your immunisation coordinator may advise contacting the vaccine manufacturer to assess potency impact based on the specific excursion conditions.
