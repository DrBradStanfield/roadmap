# Alpha

## Dosing

Trials used 300 mg in adults [1]. Some evidence suggests benefit. Take it with food, e.g. at lunch. MicroVitamin has a small dose.

## Safety

Doses above 2 g may cause upset [2]. Dr. Smith noted vs. placebo results.
